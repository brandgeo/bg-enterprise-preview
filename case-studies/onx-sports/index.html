ONX Sports builds combat sports equipment engineered around athlete protection, designed with elite MMA coach Trevor Wittman. The gear was already trusted. The website had not yet made the case.

The challenge

Strong brand, silent search footprint

A December 2025 AI brand evaluation found ONX firmly associated with the things that matter most in combat sports: protective padding engineering, wrist stability, ergonomic comfort, and injury-conscious design. Sentiment sat at 89.58% positive, comparable to elite names like Winning and Paffen Sport.

And yet ONX held 0% share of voice, with zero mentions across broad ChatGPT queries such as "best MMA gloves" and "beginner MMA gear." Fairtex, Hayabusa, and Cleto Reyes owned those entry points. ONX surfaced only when a shopper already knew the name. The expertise existed. It simply was not structured for discovery.

The strategy

Structure the expertise ONX already had

The roadmap did not manufacture a new positioning claim. It surfaced an existing strength and built a discoverable, authoritative content system around it, engineered to work across traditional search and AI retrieval alike. Two initiatives led the work.

Initiative one

The ONX Protection Standard

  • A dedicated safety and technology hub unifying padding engineering, impact dispersion, wrist alignment, and partner protection under one branded standard.
  • Product pages expanded from short sales copy into full resources: protection level, padding architecture, wrist-support systems, fit profile, FAQs, and schema.
  • Every page structured for AI retrieval with answer-first sections, comparison tables, consistent terminology, and Product, FAQ, and Person schema.
  • A hub-and-spoke model connecting safety education directly to category and product pages.
Initiative two

The Sizing and Fit Ecosystem

  • Coach and product-expert knowledge converted into dedicated sizing and measurement guides across gloves, headgear, shin guards, and wraps.
  • Content built around the two questions that stall combat sports purchases: which size to buy, and how to measure.
  • Fit guidance embedded directly on product pages, so shoppers never had to leave the buying journey.
  • High-intent, long-tail search captured around measurement, fit, and between-size decisions.

Protect the athlete so they can train longer, perform better, and recover smarter.

The central message the whole system was built to carry

The results

From niche mention to cited authority

01

Search visibility

551% more organic clicks and 857% more impressions year over year. Average Google position climbed from 14.6 to 6.2, moving ONX from the second page of results to the first. May 2026 alone saw 84% organic traffic growth year over year.

02

Conversational commerce

ChatGPT-attributed sales rose 705% in a 28-day period. By July 2026, ChatGPT described ONX gloves in the exact language the strategy set: multi-layer impact management, anatomical padding, wrist support, and a safety-first design philosophy.

03

Conversion from content

Sizing pages drove 75% of blog-attributed purchases in one 28-day window. Existing sizing and measurement pages showed 27% lower bounce and 59% higher average engagement time, evidence that fit content answered a real purchase concern.

04

Technical foundation

100% of key page templates passed Core Web Vitals. The blog generated roughly 3 million organic impressions and more than 7,000 organic visits across the six-month window, feeding both search and AI discovery.

The takeaway

A clearer, more defensible identity

Ecommerce SEO is not only keyword placement and blog volume. The highest-value move was to identify what made ONX meaningfully different and turn that knowledge into an interconnected content system that customers, search engines, and AI could all read.

The ONX Protection Standard, enriched product pages, equipment-specific safety resources, and comprehensive sizing guidance did more than add content. They gave ONX a durable source of organic growth and a story it owns.

ONX is not just a premium equipment company. It is a coach-engineered authority on how athletes train for the long term.
